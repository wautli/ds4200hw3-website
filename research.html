Computational Neuroscience & Psychophysics

At Northeastern University’s College of Science, I’m conducting independent research in cognitive neuroscience and psychophysics. My work focuses on perceptual learning and brain-training interventions, using tools like Python, MATLAB, and R to model and visualize behavioral data. I analyze how attention and memory interact during complex visual tasks and how training can enhance cognitive performance.

Health Policy & Law Research

At Northeastern’s School of Law, I developed machine learning models to predict opioid usage hotspots across Boston. This research combines data science and public health to identify patterns in prevention spending and policy effectiveness. My project was presented at RISE 2025 and demonstrated how overdose response training can reduce stigma and improve community knowledge.

Biomedical Research in Hong Kong

I also conducted research at the Hong Kong Baptist University School of Medicine, identifying key risk factors for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). I analyzed liver tissue data and explored sex-based differences in disease incidence to help improve diagnostic markers. Earlier, at HKU Med School, I supported cardiovascular research by cleaning and visualizing patient data related to heart disease biomarkers.
